## What M2 delivers, in plain words

Every business gets a **price list**: categories and services, each priced per kg (with a minimum charge), per item, or flat.
- **Price changes don't rewrite history.** Each change is a new version from a chosen time, so past orders keep the prices they were charged.
- **Extras are settings:** "modifiers" such as Express or hypoallergenic detergent.
- **VAT and the discount cap** are business settings too.
- **One module** (`apps/catalog/pricing.py`) does every price calculation, in exact decimals, rounding the total half-up to whole shillings.
- **A quote endpoint** turns lines like "6.4 kg wash and fold, Express" into a full breakdown.
- **A new business starts with the template list** of common services, prices left blank. A CSV import loads an existing list.

M2 is the **engine and its API**. Its acceptance test, from the design doc: pricing tests cover per kg, per item, minimums, modifiers, discounts, VAT, rounding and snapshots.

## Questions for you

Each has my recommendation. "Use your recommendations" is a fine answer.

| # | Question | My recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **VAT.** The design says a business setting decides whether prices include VAT and at what rate. Which rate should a VAT-registered business start with, and what is VAT charged on? | Start at **16%** (Kenya's standard rate), which the owner can change. VAT is on the laundry total **after** the discount (and, from M9, on the delivery fee too). When prices include VAT, the VAT is shown as "of which VAT"; when they don't, it's added on top. Non-registered businesses show no VAT line. |
| 2 | **Express as a flat amount.** The formula multiplies each line by (1 + percentages). How does a *flat* modifier (for example Express +KSh 200) combine? | A flat modifier is added **once per order**, not per line. Percentage modifiers apply per line and **add up**, so Express 50% plus hypoallergenic 10% is +60%, not compounded. |
| 3 | **What a modifier applies to** ("applies to" in the data model) | A chosen list of services; new modifiers default to all services. |
| 4 | **Discount basis and cap.** | A discount (percent or amount, with a required reason) comes off the laundry total after modifiers, before VAT. The cap is the business's "maximum discount %" of that total. Until the owner sets the cap, it is **0%** (no discounts), which is the safe default. |
| 5 | **The template list.** The design names wash and fold, wash and iron, ironing only, duvets, blankets, suits, curtains and shoes, but not how each is priced. | Wash and fold and Wash and iron: **per kg**. Ironing only, Duvets, Blankets, Suits and Curtains: **per item**. Shoes: **per pair**. Two categories, **Wash** and **Special items**. All start with no price and switched off until priced. |
| 6 | **Units for per-item services** | A fixed list shown after the quantity: **item, pair, set**. Per kg always shows "kg". |
| 7 | **Prices in `/business/config`.** It's public (no sign-in), so prices would be visible to anyone who knows a business's address. | Yes: prices are what customers see when booking anyway. Config lists active, priced services and modifiers. Price **history**, inactive services and overrides stay signed-in only. |
| 8 | **Who manages the catalogue.** The console table gives owners and managers "Yes" and accountants "No" for catalogue and prices. | As the table says: owners and managers edit; accountants can't. Staff and riders see prices only through quotes and config. |

## T01: Catalogue models

- **Files:**
  - `apps/catalog/models.py`:
    - `ServiceCategory` (name EN/SW, position, active);
    - `Service` (category, code unique per business, name EN/SW, pricing model, unit, position in the quick-add buttons, active);
    - `ServicePrice` (service, optional branch, unit price, minimum charge, `effective_from`, `effective_to`);
    - `PriceModifier` (name EN/SW, kind express or preference, percent **or** amount, active) and the services it applies to.
  - Migrations with `EnableTenantRLS` on every table.
  - Factories.
- **Rules in the database:**
  - money is `DecimalField(12, 2)` plus a currency;
  - the `business`-first unique constraints;
  - a check that a modifier has exactly one of percent and amount;
  - a check that `effective_to` is after `effective_from`.
- **Tests:**
  - the tenancy contract and the raw-SQL isolation test cover the new tables;
  - constraint tests.

## T02: Money settings

- **Files:** declare the money settings in the existing typed settings registry (`apps/tenancy/settings_registry.py`):
  - `vat.registered`;
  - `vat.rate` (Q1);
  - `vat.prices_include_vat`;
  - `discounts.max_percent` (Q4).
- **Tests:** types, ranges (a rate of 0–100%, a cap of 0–100%), defaults.

## T03: The pricing engine ⏸

- **Files:** `apps/catalog/pricing.py`, the only place prices are calculated.
  - **Pure functions** over plain inputs: lines (service snapshot, kg or quantity), modifiers, an optional discount, VAT settings, and a delivery fee (0 until M9).
  - **Output:** a breakdown with every line (quantity, unit price, minimum applied or not, modifiers, line amount), subtotal, flat modifiers, discount, VAT, the unrounded total, and the **total rounded half-up to whole shillings**.
  - **Rounding:** line amounts to the cent, half-up. The total to the shilling, only here.
  - **Weights** to 2 decimal places, at least 0.01 kg.
  - **Snapshots:** the result names the price version each line used, so M3 can store exactly what was charged.
- **Tests (table-driven, ≥ 90% coverage, target 100%):**
  - per kg under and over the minimum;
  - per item;
  - flat;
  - mixed orders;
  - each modifier type and combination;
  - discount by percent and by amount, at, under and over the cap, and never below zero;
  - VAT included, excluded and not registered;
  - rounding at .49 and .50, and negative-free results;
  - an independent recalculation for a set of fixed orders.
- **Stop:** you review the formulas and examples before the API is built on them.

## T04: Price versions and the catalogue services

- **Files:** `apps/catalog/services.py`, `apps/catalog/selectors.py`.
  - **Setting a price** creates a new version from now or a chosen future time and closes the previous one at that moment. Versions never overlap, checked with the service row locked, and past versions are read-only.
  - **Branch overrides** have their own versions.
  - **Turning a service off** never deletes it.
  - **Selectors:** the current price for a service and branch at a given time (branch override first, then the business price), and the price list.
  - Every change writes an `AuditLog` row with before and after values.
- **Tests:**
  - overlapping and future versions;
  - an override winning only at its branch;
  - "now" against a future price;
  - read-only history;
  - audit rows;
  - tenancy.

## T05: The template list for new businesses

## T06: Console catalogue API

- **Endpoints** (`/api/v1/console/catalog/…`, owners and managers; Q8):
  - categories and services: list, create, update, reorder, switch on/off;
  - prices: list versions, add a version, with an optional branch;
  - modifiers: list, create, update, switch on/off.
- **Rules:**
  - `Idempotency-Key` on every POST;
  - the error envelope;
  - cursor pagination;
  - audited.
- **Tests, every endpoint:** happy path, validation errors, each role, cross-business 404, idempotent replay.

## T07: CSV import

- **Endpoints:**
  - `POST /console/catalog/import/preview`: CSV text in. Out: each row as new, changed (old → new price), unchanged or invalid (with the reason), plus a fingerprint of the preview.
  - `POST /console/catalog/import/apply`: the same CSV and fingerprint, plus "effective from". Applies everything as new price versions in one transaction, or nothing.
- **Template:** `GET /console/catalog/import/template` returns the CSV template with the columns *service, category, pricing model, price, minimum*.
- **Limits:**
  - 1 MB and 1,000 rows;
  - prices validated as decimals (no floats);
  - categories and services matched by name or code.
- **Tests:**
  - a valid file;
  - every kind of bad row;
  - a fingerprint that no longer matches (the catalogue changed meanwhile);
  - partial failure leaves nothing applied;
  - cross-business.

## T08: Quote endpoint

- **Endpoint:** `POST /api/v1/quotes`, for signed-in staff, managers and owners in M2.
  - Lines in: service, and kg or quantity. Optionally the modifiers, a branch, and a discount.
  - Out: the full breakdown from `pricing.py`, with amounts as strings.
  - Saves nothing.
  - The same endpoint serves S-14's live preview (M3), A-30's calculator (M5) and, from M9, customer estimates.
- **Rules:**
  - A discount needs the person's "give discounts" right and stays within the cap. (Where rights are stored long-term is D-34, M3; M1 already stores them on the person.)
  - Inactive or unpriced services are refused with a clear message.
- **Tests:** breakdowns match `pricing.py`, permissions, cross-business, validation, throttling.

## T09: Prices in the business config

- **Files:** `GET /business/config` adds active, priced services (with quick-add positions), categories, modifiers and how VAT is shown (Q7). Changing a price clears the cached config. `openapi.yaml` and the frontend client are regenerated.
- **Frontend:**
  - the config types;
  - `format.ts` gets the price-per-unit format ("KSh 120 per kg").
  - No screens (they're M3 and M5).
- **Tests:** config contents and caching, a price change appearing, prices from another business never appearing.

## Risks

- **Rounding and VAT rules are money:** every rule is in Q1–Q4 above, and the T03 review stop exists for this.
- **Price versions racing:** two people changing one price at once. Prevented by locking the service row; tested with two connections.
- **CSV files vary in format:** commas inside quotes, a BOM from Excel, decimal commas. All handled or refused with a row-level message; tested.
